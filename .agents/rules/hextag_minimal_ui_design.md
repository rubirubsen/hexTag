# hexTag Minimalist UI & Cyber Map Design Invariants

1. **Minimalist Building Highlights:** Captured / fortified buildings must be represented by sleek, compact glowing pins (24–28px) with neon borders in the owner's color. Bulky text boxes or multi-line labels on the map are strictly prohibited to maintain maximum map visibility.
2. **Drone Orbital Behavior:** Deployed drones must circle and orbit around their designated target building or hex in a continuous, smooth radial path.
